## The problem

Average picking speed was on target, so the picking screen looked fine. Data and time spent in the warehouses showed otherwise: new hires needed at least three weeks to reach the expected speed. The screen dated from when Frichti delivered only ready-made meals, and it assumed pickers knew the whole assortment by heart.

## What I did

I redesigned the picking view around the picker, with the product information needed to find an item without knowing the warehouse.

## The result

New hires reach the target speed within their first week, and picking is 10% faster for experienced staff.
