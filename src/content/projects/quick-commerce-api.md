## The problem

Retailers wanted to offer delivery in minutes without building warehouses, a rider fleet and the software to run them. Gorillas had all three, but only for its own app.

## What I did

I initiated the project and led it with partners, operations, marketing and tech. We opened Frichti's stack through an API: warehouse allocation for each order, delivery time estimates, and real-time sync of the assortment and stock levels.

## The result

Built in 2 months, and launched with Jumbo in the Netherlands and Monoprix in France.
