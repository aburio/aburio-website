## The problem

Bike insurance is usually sold online, days after the purchase, through long forms. Bicytrust wanted to sell it where the bike is bought: at the shop counter, in the time it takes to pay.

## What I did

I designed and built the platform as the first product and tech hire: four web apps and a partner API. I led the design, wrote a good part of the code, and worked with our partner shops on the go-to-market. AI agents and low-code workflows automate our operations.

## The result

600+ partner bike shops use the platform today. Customers rate Bicytrust 4.6/5 on Trustpilot, mostly for how quick and simple signing up is.
