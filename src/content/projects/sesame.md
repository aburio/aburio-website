## The problem

Brands spend heavily on events and leave with little: no way to engage visitors on the stand, and no data to follow up afterwards.

## What I did

As co-founder, I led a designer, two developers and a sheet metal partner, from hardware design and industrialization to software. The terminal ran predictions, surveys and quizzes. A dashboard let us configure terminals remotely and analyse each event.

## The result

Total, PMU and Orange used Sesame at 300+ events. Luseed was bootstrapped and profitable, and we closed it by choice in 2020 when the model stopped scaling.
