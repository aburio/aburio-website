## Context

Bicytrust sells bike insurance online and through bike shops. I joined as the first product and tech hire, with nothing built.

## My role

I led the design of the four apps and the design system, and I write a good part of the code.

## What I built

The signup flow lets a customer insure a bike in a few minutes. The reseller space lets a shop do it at the counter, right after the sale. The client space covers contracts and claims. A partner API connects the platform to third parties.

## Result

600+ partner bike shops use the platform today. Customers rate Bicytrust 4.6/5 on Trustpilot, mostly for how quick and simple signing up is.
