## Context

I'm a member of this flying club at Lyon Bron, and I rebuilt its website as a volunteer.

## What I built

The old site was organized around the club. The new one starts from the visitor's project: discover, learn, fly, progress. Volunteers edit content through Keystatic, connected to GitHub, and the site runs on Vercel's nonprofit plan.

## Result

Clicks and impressions from Google search are up 150% for August to October 2026, compared with the same three months in 2025 (Google Search Console).

## Why Astro

I wanted something light, with a rigid structure, content that is easy to edit and no database. Astro with Keystatic gives exactly that, and the club pays nothing for hosting.
