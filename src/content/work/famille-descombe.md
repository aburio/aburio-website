## Context

Famille Descombe has been making wine in Beaujolais and Chablis since 1905. Their WordPress site took 30 seconds to load and was painful to update.

## What I built

I rebuilt the site with Lovable, in French and English. I also built an admin where the team adds a wine in a few fields, and AI suggests food pairings and crops the photos.

## Result

Rebuilt in 3 days. Pages now load in 2 seconds, and the team updates the wine catalogue on its own.

## Why Lovable

I wanted the family to be able to edit content and create new pages without me. Lovable makes that simpler for them than an AI agent, a GitHub repository and separate hosting.
